Privacy-safe radar gesture recognition on microcontrollers

2026
  • Recognises 12 hand gestures from 3 UWB radars at 89.6% macro-F1 on users the model never saw (leave-one-subject-out, 8 participants). Published 97–99% results on this dataset use random splits that leak the same person into training and test.
  • Causal, streaming pre-processing (clutter removal, Hilbert envelope) shrank the input 5.9× and lifted a random-forest baseline from 70.6 to 89.9 macro-F1.
  • Quantized a 24K-parameter CNN to a 30 KB INT8 model (0.3-point loss), benchmarked on 6 STM32 boards: 22.9 ms on a Cortex-M7.
  • Privacy audit: an attacker identifies users with 98.7% accuracy from raw radar, but only 13.4% (chance 12.8%) from on-device labels.
